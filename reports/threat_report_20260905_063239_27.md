## Salesforce Hardening

- Audit integration/API user permissions to ensure marketing automation tools only have field-level access to contact data necessary for newsletter distribution (data minimization).
- Enable and review Salesforce Event Monitoring / Transaction Security policies to alert on large report or list-view exports of Contact objects.
- Periodically review connected apps and OAuth scopes tied to marketing/newsletter integrations to ensure they cannot access broader CRM data than required.
